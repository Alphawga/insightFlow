# Project Brief: InsightFlow

## Core Requirements
- A personal brand and product launch over a 90-day timeline
- Development of a SaaS product while maintaining full-time employment 
- Limited daily development time (4-5 hours)
- Integration of product development with content creation

## Project Goals
1. Complete MVP by March 31st with reduced scope focused on essential features
2. Attract 5-10 committed beta users by April 15th
3. Acquire at least 3 paying or committed users by May 15th
4. Establish either promising job opportunities or investor interest by May 31st
5. Build personal brand while documenting the build-in-public journey

## Scope Definition
- MVP with ruthlessly prioritized features (eliminating all nice-to-haves)
- Content creation derived directly from development documentation
- Strategic use of limited time blocks with maximum efficiency
- Dual-path advancement strategy (job opportunities and potential investment)

## Success Metrics
1. Completion of essential MVP features on timeline
2. User acquisition and retention rates
3. Content engagement across platforms
4. Growth in professional opportunities
5. Product-market fit indicators

## Timeline Overview
- **March**: MVP Completion Phase
- **April**: Validation & Iteration Phase
- **May**: Growth & Outreach Phase

This document serves as the foundation for all project activities and decisions.
