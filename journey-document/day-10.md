90 Days. 4-5 Hours Per Day. One Developer.

We're currently building the onboarding flow for InsightFlow, our Google Ads analytics platform, while juggling full-time work.

The most challenging part? Creating a seamless Google OAuth integration that will pass verification while maintaining a beautiful UI to guide users through each step.

Our onboarding will handle account creation, platform connection, and report customization in minutes instead of hours.

The biggest lesson so far: When building with limited time, every decision matters. We're focusing only on what provides core value and cutting everything else.

For those building their own products on limited time:
1. Focus on just 25 minutes of progress daily
2. Document everything as you build
3. When using Google APIs, always configure test users properly first

Still much work ahead, but progress is steady! What are you building with your limited time?
